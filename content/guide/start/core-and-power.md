# Core and Power

Forma has two plans: Forma Core and Forma Power. Both use the same coach-first app. The difference is whether you want Garmin data and optional online AI help.

## Forma Core

Core is everything on your phone. You can talk or type to the coach, log food and weight, train, check in, and see your reports and charts.

Speech recognition happens on the iPhone. Your coach memories stay on the phone too. Backup goes to your own private iCloud.

Apple Health is optional. Forma only reads from it; it does not write to it.

## Forma Power

Power adds:

- **Garmin data** for the watch information Forma can use.
- **Free-form questions** with online AI help.
- **Meal ideas** with online AI help.
- **Meal photos and descriptions** that online AI can help interpret.

Online AI help is opt-in. You choose whether to use it.

## The watch-data boundary

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service. That boundary applies even when you use Power and turn online AI help on.

This is separate from choosing to send a free-form question, meal idea request, meal photo or meal description for online AI help.

## Which one should you use?

Choose Core if you want the coach and your data on your phone without online AI help.

Consider Power if Garmin data matters to you, or you want the optional online help with broader questions and meals. You do not need Power to speak to the coach or to use the everyday commands in this guide.
