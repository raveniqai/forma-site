# Your first week

Your first week is for learning how you like to use Forma. Speak when that is easiest. Type when you are somewhere quiet. Keep the requests small.

## Day one: meet your coach

Finish the first conversation about your goal, body details and training week. Look at the calorie and protein targets and the first session that Forma reveals.

Then try one everyday request, such as `log espresso` or `I weigh 78.4`.

## Day two: try the morning check-in

Choose the word that fits: Wrecked, Flat, Normal, Good or Strong. If a number is easier, say `I feel 7`.

Be honest rather than hopeful. The check-in is for you and your coach, not a score to win.

## Day three: log a meal your way

Say or type what you ate. For example, `log breakfast 107 g cucumber`.

Open **Fuel** from the grid menu if you want to see the food view. You do not need to open it every time you log something.

## Day four: train or move a little

If it is a training day, say `start training`. Ask for `warm up` first if you want one.

If time is tight, say `I only have 35 minutes`. On a non-training day, `desk reset` or `stretch` can be a small way to break up sitting.

## Day five: ask about a number

Tap one of the six home numbers for its chart. Or ask `how did I sleep this week` or `resting heart rate this month`.

A single number is only a snapshot. Give yourself time to build a trend.

## Day six: look at training progress

After you have some training history, try `show my bench progress`, `any new records` or `show my training volume`.

Early on, there may not be much to show. That is normal.

## Day seven: review the week

Ask `how was my week` or `am I on track`. Then open **Reports** from the grid menu for the short coach summary and on-track card.

Notice what felt easy. Keep that part for next week. You do not need a perfect logging streak to make Forma useful.
