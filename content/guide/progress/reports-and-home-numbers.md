# Reports and the numbers on your home screen

Forma gives you two levels of detail. The home screen keeps six numbers close. Reports gives you a coach summary and the wider picture.

## The six home numbers

The quiet strip on your home screen shows:

- **This week** for a quick weekly reference.
- **Protein** for your protein number.
- **Weight** for your weight record.
- **Sleep** for your sleep information.
- **HRV** for heart-rate variability.
- **Resting heart rate** for that recovery number.

Tap any one of the six to open its chart. You do not need to open Reports first.

## Ask instead of tapping

The coach can answer direct questions about the same part of your life. Try:

- `how did I sleep this week`
- `resting heart rate this month`
- `show my body fat`
- `show my training volume`

Use the coach when you want words and context. Use a chart when you want to see the shape over time.

## Open Reports

1. Open the grid menu at the top right of the coach screen.
2. Choose **Reports**.
3. Read the short coach summary first.
4. Look at your on-track card below it.

The on-track card brings together your **fitness score**, **weight**, **muscle progress** and **body trends**.

## Open the full Progress screen

Choose **Full report** in Reports. This opens the full Progress screen for a deeper look.

Reports and the home numbers are different views, not different records. Use whichever level of detail suits the moment.

> **Note:** Numbers and scores are estimates and need context. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
