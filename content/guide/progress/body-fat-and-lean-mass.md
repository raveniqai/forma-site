# Body fat and muscle progress

To ask about body fat, say or type `show my body fat` on the coach screen.

Reports also places muscle progress and body trends on your on-track card. This keeps them beside your fitness score and weight rather than treating one measurement as the whole story.

## Look at direction, not just a number

Body-composition readings can vary. The method, device and conditions can all affect what you see.

Use the same source consistently where you can, and give the trend time to develop. Avoid drawing a big conclusion from one reading.

## Add training context

Muscle progress is easier to think about alongside training. Ask:

- `show my bench progress`
- `any new records`
- `show my training volume`

These are different views. A change in one does not automatically explain a change in another.

## Optional connected data

Apple Health is optional and read-only. Forma Power can add Garmin data.

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service, including when optional online AI help is on.

> **Note:** Body-composition information is an estimate, not a diagnosis. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
