# The fitness score

Your fitness score appears on the on-track card in Reports. It sits beside weight, muscle progress and body trends so you can read it as part of a wider picture.

## Find the score

1. Open the grid menu at the top right of the coach screen.
2. Choose **Reports**.
3. Read the short coach summary.
4. Find the fitness score on your on-track card.

Choose **Full report** when you want to open the full Progress screen.

## Use it as a summary

The score is one way to look at your information. It is not the only measure of a good week, and it is not a medical test.

Read it beside the things you can observe directly: your training, food record, weight, check-ins and recovery numbers.

## Ask the coach about the wider picture

Try `am I on track` for a direct question about your goal. Ask `how was my week` for a broader review, or `any patterns` when you want to explore what Forma has recorded.

If you want more detail about one area, ask a narrower question such as `show my training volume` or `how did I sleep this week`.

> **Note:** Forma gives information and estimates, not medical advice. A score cannot diagnose your health or fitness.
