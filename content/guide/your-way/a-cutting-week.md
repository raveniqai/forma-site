# A week focused on fat loss

If fat loss is your goal, begin with the targets Forma revealed during setup. Keep the week about useful information, not perfection.

## Log the basics

Tell the coach what you eat with short requests such as `log breakfast 107 g cucumber` and `log espresso`.

Record weigh-ins with `I weigh 78.4`, using your real reading and unit. Protein is in the home-number strip when you want a quick glance.

## Ask about the target

Say `why is my calorie target what it is` whenever the number needs explaining.

Your setup answers about your goal, body details and training week are part of the starting context. Check them if something seems off.

## Review more than the scale

Ask `am I on track` and `how was my week`. Reports shows weight and body trends beside muscle progress and your fitness score.

One weigh-in is a snapshot. Training, food, recovery and the longer trend all add context.

## Keep health ahead of the target

Do not use Forma to push through illness, pain or symptoms. Speak to a qualified professional before making major dietary changes if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant, or have concerns about food or your body.

Forma gives information, not medical advice.
