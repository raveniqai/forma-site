# Ways to judge progress

No single number has to carry the whole story. Forma lets you choose a quick glance, a direct question or a fuller report.

## Glance at the home strip

The six numbers are this week, protein, weight, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. Tap one for its chart.

This is useful when you want a quick check without starting a longer review.

## Ask one clear question

Use the coach for the part you care about:

- `show my bench progress`
- `any new records`
- `show my training volume`
- `how did I sleep this week`
- `resting heart rate this month`
- `show my body fat`

Narrow questions make it easier to look at one trend without confusing it with another.

## Read Reports

Open **Reports** from the grid menu. Start with the short coach summary, then read the on-track card.

The card includes your fitness score, weight, muscle progress and body trends. Choose **Full report** for the full Progress screen.

## Review the week

Ask `how was my week`, `am I on track` or `any patterns` when you want to step back.

Use more than one kind of evidence. Food, training, weight and recovery can move differently. A pattern is a clue, not proof of cause.

> **Note:** Charts, targets and scores are estimates. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
