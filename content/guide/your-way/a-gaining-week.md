# A week focused on building

If your goal is to build, give the coach a realistic starting picture during setup. Your training days, where you train and your body details all belong in that first conversation.

## Start with the revealed plan

At the end of setup, look at your calorie and protein targets and your first session.

When you train, say `start training`. When you eat, use short requests such as `log breakfast 107 g cucumber`. Protein is also one of the six home numbers.

## Look at training over time

Ask `show my bench progress`, `any new records` and `show my training volume` as your history grows.

One hard session is not the whole picture. A few weeks of consistent information are more useful than chasing a record every day.

## Include recovery

Use the morning check-in when it helps. Ask `how did I sleep this week` or tap sleep, HRV and resting heart rate for their charts.

Recovery numbers are information, not targets to perfect. Read them alongside how you feel.

## Review the whole week

Ask `how was my week` and `am I on track`. In Reports, your on-track card includes muscle progress and body trends as well as weight and the fitness score.

Forma gives information, not medical advice. Speak to a qualified professional about major changes to your diet or training when health concerns apply.
