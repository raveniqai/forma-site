# Start a training session

When you are ready, say or type `start training` on the coach screen. You do not need to find a separate start screen first.

You can also open **Train** from the grid menu at the top right.

## Your first session

The first-run conversation ends by revealing your first session, along with your calorie and protein targets. Start with that session when you are ready.

Your setup answers about training days and where you train give Forma its starting context.

## Before you begin

Tell the coach anything that changes today:

- `I only have 35 minutes`
- `equipment taken?`
- `warm up`

Short context is enough. You do not need to write a long explanation.

## During and after training

Keep the coach close for what you need. Ask about equipment, use **Train** for the training view, and ask for `cool down` when you finish.

Later, you can ask `show my bench progress`, `any new records` or `show my training volume` to look back on the work you logged.

> **Careful:** Stop if something hurts or feels wrong. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
