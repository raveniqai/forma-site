# Past sessions and reports

The clock button at the top of Train opens History & reports: every session you have logged, the reports built from them, and the tools to correct the record. Use it after training to check what you did, fix a mistake, or see where your lifts are going.

## When to use it

- You want to look back at a session: its lifts, its sets, and how it went.
- You logged a set wrongly, or skipped answering for a set, and want the record to say what really happened.
- You trained but forgot to log it, and want the day filled in.
- You want the longer view: sets per muscle, your lifts over time, your weekly reviews.

## Step by step

1. Tap the clock button at the top right of Train. The History & reports screen opens, with **Recent sessions** at the top.
2. Tap **All** on the sessions card, which shows your session count, to open History: every session, grouped by month. Tap a session to open it.
3. A session opens on its summary, then its sets, grouped by lift. Tap a set to correct it: change the weight or the reps, mark it as a warm-up, or set how many reps you had left. Tap **Save**.
4. To remove what you logged, open the set and tap **Delete set**. To delete a whole lift, use the three-dot menu next to it.
5. To add a missed day, tap the plus at the top of History, labelled **Add a missed session**. Pick the day: today or any day in the last three weeks. Tap **Choose a lift** and enter its sets.
6. Back on History & reports, tap **Sets per muscle** for this week's sets per muscle and the last 8 weeks, and **Your lifts** for every lift with its best set. Tap a lift to see its trend.

If you already logged a session on the day you pick, the lift goes into that session. Forma never creates a second workout for one day. A day with no session yet gets a new one, which shows without a duration because how long you trained is unknown.

## The reports

History & reports collects more than sessions:

- **Sets per muscle** — each muscle's sets this week, with the last 8 weeks charted. The screen notes that 10 to 20 sets a week per muscle builds muscle.
- **Your lifts** — every lift you have trained, with your best set and how many sessions it appears in. Tap one for its trend.
- **Weigh-ins** and **Measurements** — your body records.
- **Weekly reviews** — one line per week on how it went.
- **Coach decisions** — when the coach changed a target, why, with an Undo where it can be reversed.

Which rows appear depends on the coaches you have on. The Food diary row only appears with the diet coach on.

## Good to know

- Changing a past set updates your recovery readings, your next suggested weights and your insights. Corrections keep the record right.
- Deleting a session asks first, and it cannot be undone. Forma warns you that it changes your recovery, your next weights and your insights.
- Warm-up sets are shown but never counted as volume, recovery or records.
- Most edits can be taken back for a few seconds with the Undo bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.
- Your first session sets your starting weights. Once you have repeated a lift on two different days, its trend appears in Your lifts.

> **Note:** A deleted session is gone for good. If you only meant to remove one lift or one set, delete that instead.
