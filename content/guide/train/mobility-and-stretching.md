# Mobility and stretching

You can ask the coach for a small amount of movement without starting a full training session. Use the same coach screen by voice or text.

## Before training

Say `warm up` when you want to prepare for your session.

Notice how your body feels as you move. A warm-up is a chance to ease in, not a demand to push harder.

## After training

Say `cool down` when the session is finished. Ask for `stretch` if you want a quieter finish.

You do not need to rush from your last movement straight back into a busy day.

## During a desk-bound day

Say `desk reset` when you have been sitting and want a short break. It can be a small pause even when a full session does not fit.

You can also ask for `stretch` on its own. Keep it comfortable and work within what feels right for you.

## Keep the request in context

Use `start training` for a session and these shorter requests for the movement around it. On a busy day, telling the coach `I only have 35 minutes` adds useful context too.

> **Careful:** Stretching and mobility work are not treatment for an injury. Stop if anything hurts and ask a qualified professional for advice.
