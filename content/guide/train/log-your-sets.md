# Warm up, train and cool down

A session can have a simple beginning, middle and end. Ask the coach for the part you need instead of searching through menus.

## Warm up

Say or type `warm up` before you train.

Use the warm-up to ease into movement and pay attention to how you feel. It is not a test of how hard you can go.

## Train

Say `start training` when you are ready for the session. Open **Train** from the grid menu if you prefer the full training view.

If the day is not going to plan, tell the coach. `I only have 35 minutes` and `equipment taken?` are useful starting points.

## Cool down

Say `cool down` after the work is done. You can also ask for `stretch` when you want a gentler finish.

There is no need to rush this part. Give yourself a moment before heading back to the rest of the day.

## Keep it comfortable

A warm-up or cool-down should not be used to push through pain. Stop if something feels wrong and speak to a qualified professional about an injury or symptoms.
