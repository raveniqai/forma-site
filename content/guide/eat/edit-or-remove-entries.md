# Keep food logging simple

Food logging works best when it fits the meal you are having. A short request while it is fresh is easier than rebuilding the whole day later.

## Log as you go

Tell the coach after breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack. Use simple requests such as `log breakfast 107 g cucumber` and `log espresso`.

You do not need to wait until every part of the day is complete.

## Review the day in Fuel

Open the grid menu at the top right and choose **Fuel**. This is the full food view when you want to look beyond the conversation.

Compare what is there with what you actually ate. Pay particular attention to amounts, because a food name alone does not describe the portion.

## Check your targets

The first-run conversation reveals your starting calorie and protein targets. Later, ask `why is my calorie target what it is` when you want an explanation.

The protein number in the home strip gives you a quick reference. Tap it for its chart.

## Do not chase perfection

A food log is information for you and your coach. It does not need to be a perfect record of every gram to be useful.

Ask `am I on track` or `how was my week` for a wider view instead of judging one meal on its own.
