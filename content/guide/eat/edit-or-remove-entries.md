# Change or remove an entry

A day's food record is not fixed once you save it. Every logged line can be corrected, moved to another meal, or removed. Your weekly numbers follow the change.

## When to use it

- You got a portion wrong, or left a number out.
- Breakfast ended up logged as lunch.
- You logged the same thing twice, or logged food on a day you meant to skip.

## Step by step

### Fix the numbers

1. On Fuel, find the line under its meal.
2. Tap the line. The entry opens with its numbers.
3. Change the amount, or the protein, carbs and fat. The calories are counted from those numbers; there is no box to type them into.
4. Tap **Save changes**. **Cancel** throws your edits away.

### Move it to another meal

1. Swipe the line to the left.
2. Tap **Meal**.
3. In *Move to which meal?*, tap the meal it belongs to.

You can also tap the three dots at the end of the line (or press and hold the line) and choose **Change meal**. Either way, only the meal changes: the foods, the numbers and the time logged stay as they are.

### Remove it

1. Swipe the line to the left. Or tap the three dots at the end of the line, or press and hold the line.
2. Tap **Delete** (with the swipe), **Remove** (in the menu), or open the entry and tap **Delete this entry**. Swiping the line all the way removes it at once.
3. A message at the bottom of the screen offers **Undo** for a few seconds. Tap it and the line comes back exactly as it was.

The same three-dot menu also offers **Edit** and **Keep as a meal**, so a day can be corrected or saved from one place; see [Saved meals and repeating a meal](/guide/eat/saved-meals/) for that.

### Close or reopen the day

1. To close a day sooner, use the menu at the top right of Fuel: **Close today** for today, **Close this day** for a past day.
2. If fewer than two lines are logged, Forma asks first. Tap **Done anyway**.
3. A closed day shows a closed line with a **Reopen** button. Tap it to open the day again until you close it.

## Good to know

> **Note:** A day closes by itself at 21:00, if something is logged in it. The close item only appears on a day with at least one logged line that is not closed yet. A day with nothing logged is never closed, by hand or by itself.

> **Note:** Weekly numbers lean on closed days: when at least three of the logged days in a week are closed, only the closed ones are averaged, so a half-logged day does not drag the number down. Until then, every day you logged is averaged. See [Your week](/guide/eat/your-week/).

> **Limit:** You can log and change days up to 14 days back; older days are read-only and show what you logged with nothing to tap. Step between days with the arrows at the top of Fuel, and tap the date to open a calendar.

> **Note:** Moving a line keeps the time you logged it. The entry screen has no field for the time: a line keeps its clock time until you remove it and log the food again. Only a line logged today carries the time you log it at; on a day before today, Forma times new lines to that meal's usual hour.

> **Careful:** **Undo** disappears after a few seconds. After that, a removed line is gone for good.

> **Tip:** The full three-dot menu is also available as a press-and-hold on the line, and VoiceOver users have the same actions as named actions on the row (*Change meal* and *Remove*).
