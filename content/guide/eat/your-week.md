# Your week

A weekly review can be one question. On the coach screen, say or type `how was my week`.

## Ask the question you actually have

Use a broad request when you want a summary:

- `how was my week`
- `am I on track`
- `any patterns`

Use a narrower request when one number matters:

- `show my training volume`
- `how did I sleep this week`
- `show my bench progress`

You can follow a quiet week with the same questions. Just remember that less logging means less information for the coach to use.

## Open Reports

Open **Reports** from the grid menu at the top right. It starts with a short coach summary.

Below that, the on-track card brings together your fitness score, weight, muscle progress and body trends. Choose **Full report** to open the full Progress screen.

## Use more than one signal

Food and protein matter, but they are not the whole week. Training, weight, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate can add context.

The six home numbers give you a quick view. Tap one for its chart, then return to the coach with a question if you want help reading it.

> **Tip:** Treat the weekly review as information for next week, not a grade for last week.
