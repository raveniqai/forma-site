# Meals, snacks and drinks

You do not need a different workflow for every kind of food. Tell the coach what you had and include the meal or amount when it helps.

## Breakfast

Use a direct request such as `log breakfast 107 g cucumber`.

Naming the meal makes your intention clear. Naming the amount adds useful detail when you know it.

## Other meals and snacks

Use the same coach screen later in the day. Say or type what you ate in the same plain way.

You do not have to save a special meal template or remember a command for each food. Start with the food itself.

## Drinks

A short `log espresso` is enough to begin logging that drink.

Coffee, water breaks and snacks do not require you to open **Fuel** first. The full Fuel view is there under the grid menu when you want it.

## When you want meal help

With Forma Power, optional online AI help can provide meal ideas and help with meal photos and descriptions. You choose whether to turn it on.

Core still lets you talk or type to the coach and keeps everything on your phone.
