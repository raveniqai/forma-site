# Saved meals and repeating a meal

A saved meal is a meal you logged once and Forma remembered. The next time you eat it, one tap writes the whole line back into that day, so you do not search the same foods again.

## When to use it

- You eat the same breakfast most mornings and want it logged before the day starts.
- A dinner you cook the same way each week comes back again and again.
- You usually log the same foods together, and searching them one by one takes too long.

## Step by step

### Save a meal once

1. Log the meal the way you normally do (one way is a search; see [Log food: search, scan or by hand](/guide/eat/log-food/)).
2. On Fuel, find one of its lines in the day's list of meals, in the card headed **Today's meals**.
3. Tap the three dots at the end of that line.
4. Tap **Keep as a meal**.

A note at the bottom of the screen confirms the save and shows the name it was saved under. The name comes from the foods on that line: two foods give "one and the other", three or more give the first food and a count.

> **Note:** You also reach *Keep as a meal* by tapping a logged line itself, which opens the entry, and tapping **Keep as a meal** at the bottom of that entry. Both routes save the same thing.

### Log it again

1. Next time you eat that meal, open Fuel and tap the plus at the top right (**Add food**).
2. Tap your meal under **Saved meals**. It is logged at once into the meal named under *Logging to*, and a note with an **Undo** appears at the bottom.

Saved meals are listed with the name, the calories and the protein. The ones you log most often come first, and the sheet shows the six most-logged.

### Or repeat the whole meal

Repeating works without saving anything first:

1. On Fuel, a meal with nothing logged in it shows a **Repeat** button with the calories of the last time you logged that meal. Tap it, and the whole meal is copied into today, one fresh line for each line you logged then.
2. In the **Add food** sheet, a section named after the meal — *Repeat lunch*, *Repeat breakfast* — shows the same thing: the most recent day first, and **More** brings up the other recent days.

## Good to know

> **Note:** A saved meal is a copy of the line you tapped, with the foods and numbers it had at that moment. Editing the saved copy never changes the day you logged first, and fixing that day never changes the saved copy.

> **Note:** *Keep as a meal* saves the one line you tap, with the foods that line holds. Foods you logged as separate lines belong to separate entries, so each needs its own save.

> **Tip:** A saved meal remembers what you ate, not when. Pick the meal you want in the *Logging to* menu on Fuel before you open the sheet, so a usual breakfast can go back in as lunch. The meal button in the top right of the sheet changes it there too.

> **Limit:** The **Saved meals** and **Repeat** sections show while the search box is empty or holds a single character. Once you type two characters or more, they give way to search results. Repeat looks back over the last 60 days, and only at days before the one you are on.
