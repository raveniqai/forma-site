# Your calorie and protein targets

Your first conversation with Forma ends with a reveal of your calorie and protein targets. Your first session appears with them.

## The answers behind the starting plan

During setup, the coach asks about your goal, height, weight, sex, birth date, training days and where you train.

Those answers give Forma the context for your starting targets. Check that your answers reflect you, especially the body details and goal.

## Ask why

When you want an explanation, say or type `why is my calorie target what it is`.

This is usually easier than trying to reverse-engineer the number from a chart. Ask the question on the coach screen whenever the target surprises you.

## See protein at a glance

Protein is one of the six numbers in the quiet home strip. Tap it to open its chart.

For the full food and target view, open **Fuel** from the grid menu.

## Use targets as information

A target is an estimate, not a medical prescription. Your logged food, weight and other records help you look at what happens over time.

Ask `am I on track` for a direct check, or `how was my week` for a wider review.

> **Note:** Forma gives information, not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making major changes to how you eat if you have a medical condition or other health concern.
