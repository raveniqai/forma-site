# Supplements

The Supplements section on the Fuel tab is a list of what you take, with a one-tap dose. It keeps a record and lets a dose count toward your macros where it should. Forma does not advise what to take or how much.

## When to use it

- You take something most days, such as creatine or whey, and want a record of it.
- You want a protein shake to count toward your daily protein target.
- You want a nudge on the days you forget a usual supplement.

## Step by step

1. Open Fuel and scroll to **Supplements**.
2. Tap **Add a supplement**.
3. Fill it in whichever way suits you: tap **Scan the barcode** to read a packet, tap a name under **Common supplements**, or tap **Type it in**.
4. Where photo reading is set up and allowed on your phone, **Photograph the label** reads the Supplement Facts panel. You check what it read before anything is saved.
5. On the form, check the **Name**, pick a **Kind** (Protein, Creatine, Caffeine, Vitamin or Other) and, if you like, type a **Usual dose**: the field suggests "5 g or 2 capsules".
6. Leave the **Macros per dose (optional)** fields blank unless the supplement has some. If what you type is not a number, the form says "Use numbers only, for example 24 or 1.5." A protein supplement with nothing entered is treated as unknown, never guessed as zero.
7. Tap **Add and log the first dose**.

From then on, tapping the supplement's name on Fuel logs a dose. The green number on it counts today's doses.

> **Note:** Doses are logged for today only. If you are looking at an earlier day, the list shows that day's counts but does not log new ones.

## What each kind does

The form describes each Kind in one line:

- **Protein**: counts toward your daily protein target.
- **Creatine** and **Vitamin**: no macros.
- **Caffeine**: timed against your sleep.
- **Other**: no note, no special behaviour.

## More options per supplement

Press and hold a supplement's name for its menu:

- **Log a dose**. When the supplement has a usual dose, the item names your usual dose instead.
- **Out of stock** and **Restocked**. Marking one out of stock shows a short sentence about your record of taking it, and its row is dimmed until it is back. Logging a dose while it is out of stock marks it back in stock.
- **Stopped**, and **Take it again** to restart a stopped one.
- **Undo last dose**.
- **Remove (keeps history)**. The supplement leaves the list but its past doses stay.

If you scan or photograph a supplement you already have, Forma does not add a second one. It offers to log a dose of the one you own, or, if the old one was removed, to add it again.

> **Note:** The names under Common supplements fill in a name, a Kind, a typical dose and, for some of them, typical macros. They are typical values you can edit, not a recommendation to take them.

> **Limit:** Scanning a barcode or reading a label photo is an aid, not a check. The numbers are what you leave in the form, so correct anything that was read wrongly before you save.

## A reminder when you forget

If you want a nudge when you have not logged a usual supplement:

1. Open Settings, then **Reminders & notifications**.
2. Open **Reminders** and turn on **Supplements**.

It only reminds you when your usual ones are missing for the day. On a day you logged them, it says nothing.
