# Supplements

You can log a supplement from the same coach screen as your food. Say or type `log my creatine`.

## Keep it simple

Tell the coach when you take it. You do not need to open **Fuel** first.

If you also want to log a drink such as coffee, use a separate short request like `log espresso`.

## Review your day

Open **Fuel** from the grid menu when you want the fuller view of your food and targets.

The home-number strip includes protein. Tap it for its chart when you want to see more than today's number.

## Forma does not prescribe supplements

Logging creatine or another supplement is a record of what you chose to take. It is not a recommendation that you should take it, or how much you should take.

Ask a doctor, pharmacist or another qualified professional about supplements, interactions and your own health. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
