# When the coach does not understand

A request can be too broad, missing a detail, or hard to hear. You do not need to learn a special command language. A small clarification is usually the best next step.

## Try a shorter request

Use one action at a time. These are reliable starting points:

- `log breakfast 107 g cucumber`
- `log espresso`
- `log my creatine`
- `I weigh 78.4`
- `start training`
- `how did I sleep this week`

Include the food, amount, lift or period you mean when that detail matters.

## Type instead

Speech is recognised on the iPhone. If a noisy room makes speaking difficult, type the same words on the coach screen.

Typing is also useful when you want to check a number such as `78.4` before sending it.

## Ask what Forma can do

Say or type `what can you do` for help from inside the app.

You can also use [Talk to your coach](/guide/coaches/talk-to-your-coach/) for a grouped cheat sheet of everyday requests.

## When you want a broader answer

Forma Power adds optional online AI help for free-form questions. Core still handles the everyday on-phone coaching described in this guide.

Do not add private medical details just to make a request clearer. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
