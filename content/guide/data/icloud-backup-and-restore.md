# iCloud backup and restore

Forma keeps a copy of your data in your own private iCloud, and keeps the previous copy too. A lost phone or a mistake does not cost you your history. Everything works with no connection, and the backup catches up when you are back online.

## When to use it

- You want to check that your data is being backed up, or run a backup now.
- You have a new iPhone and want to bring your history with you.
- You deleted something by mistake and want to go back to an earlier copy.
- Forma shows a small dot on the gear at the top of Today. It appears when backups have failed or been missing for three days.

## Step by step

### To check or run a backup

1. Open Forma, then open Settings.
2. Tap **Data & backup**.
3. Read the iCloud status line. It says whether you are backed up and when, or what is in the way.
4. If you want a backup now, tap **Back up now**.

The status line can say:

- **On · last backed up [time]** — the last backup succeeded. Everything on this phone as of that moment is safe in iCloud.
- **Backing up…** — Forma is copying a snapshot to iCloud right now.
- **On · waiting for a connection** — everything is saved on this phone. The backup runs the next time you are online.
- **On · not backed up yet** — your first backup runs on its own the next time you open Forma, or tap Back up now.
- **iCloud is full** — this appears as a message under the status line: there is no room left in your iCloud storage for the backup. Your data is safe on the phone. Free up space in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud and Forma will carry on.

> **Careful:** If iCloud already holds a fuller copy than this phone, Forma will not overwrite it. It says so and offers two choices: **Replace the backup with this phone's data**, or **Keep the backup as it is**. Choose with care; if the backup is right, restore from it instead.

### To restore on a new phone

1. Install Forma and sign in to the same Apple Account.
2. On the first screen, tap **Restoring a backup?**.
3. Tap **Restore from iCloud**. Forma downloads the latest copy; the screen says how many records were downloaded when it is done.
4. Force-quit and reopen Forma. The restored data is now in place.

If Forma is already installed (no first screen), open Settings, tap **Data & backup**, then **Restore from backup**.

### To restore an earlier copy

1. Open Settings, then **Data & backup**, then **Restore from backup**.
2. Choose either **Latest backup** or **Earlier copy**. Each row shows its date and how many entries it holds.
3. Tap **Download and apply at next launch**.
4. Force-quit and reopen Forma.

> **Note:** Nothing changes until you restart Forma. You get a message when the download is done, telling you how many entries were prepared.

## Good to know

- Your data lives on this phone. The copy in iCloud is yours, in your own iCloud account. Forma works with no connection; the backup catches up when you are back online.
- Restoring replaces what is on the iPhone with what is in that backup. Anything logged since then is replaced. Your current data is kept aside, not deleted.
- The earlier copy is the backup from before the latest one. It is kept so that one bad backup cannot leave you without copies.
- Forma never overwrites a fuller backup by itself. If a backup is refused, the app asks you which side is right.
- Offline is a normal state. If the backup cannot reach iCloud, Forma says so and tries again later.

> **Limit:** If your iPhone is not signed in to iCloud, backups cannot run until you sign in. Your data is still saved on the phone.

If a backup message says iCloud is off, see the next steps:

- **Sign in to iCloud to back up:** open the iOS Settings app, sign in with your Apple Account, then return to Forma and tap Back up now.
- **iCloud is turned off for Forma:** iCloud is restricted on this phone, often by Screen Time or a device-management profile. Ask whoever manages the phone.
- **iCloud is full:** free up space in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud. Your data stays safe on the phone.
- **"Can't check iCloud right now" or "iCloud is busy":** temporary. Forma tries again the next time you open it.
