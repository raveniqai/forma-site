# Apple Health

Apple Health is optional in Forma. If you choose to connect it, Forma reads from Health. It never writes to Health.

## Read-only means read-only

Forma can use permitted Health information without changing the records in Apple Health. Your Forma requests and logs do not get written back into Health by the app.

You can use Forma without connecting Apple Health. Talking to the coach, typing logs and using the main app do not depend on it.

## Recovery and body numbers

Health data can add context to the numbers you already see in Forma. The home strip includes weight, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, and each opens a chart when tapped.

You can also ask questions such as `how did I sleep this week`, `resting heart rate this month` and `show my body fat`.

## Change your mind

Apple Health access is your choice. You can review Health permissions in Apple's settings if you want to change what Forma may read.

Removing access does not turn Forma into a different app. You can continue to use the coach by speaking or typing.

## Health data and AI

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service. This remains true with Forma Power and optional online AI help.

Forma gives information, not medical advice. Health readings are not a diagnosis.
