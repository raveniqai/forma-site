# What your coach remembers

The coach can remember a few things you tell it so you do not have to repeat them. These memories are stored on the phone only.

## What it may remember

The coach can keep context such as:

- a food you avoid;
- being sick;
- travelling;
- a busy week.

This is a small set of useful context, not a hidden biography. Your ordinary requests and logs do not mean you have to give the coach more personal detail than you want to.

## Ask what it knows

On the coach screen, say or type `what do you know about me`.

This is a quick way to start when you are curious about the information behind a response.

## See the memories yourself

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Open **Data & backup**.
3. Open **What the coach remembers**.

You will see the memories the coach has kept. Review them whenever you like.

## Delete a memory

Use the delete option in **What the coach remembers** for anything you do not want the coach to keep.

Deleting a memory is separate from deleting all Forma data. You can remove one piece of coach context without wiping your logs.

> **Note:** These memories stay on the phone. They are not needed for online AI help, and nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.
