# Coach summaries, patterns and experiments

Sometimes one number is enough. Sometimes you want to know what the last few days mean. The coach can help you step back.

## Start with your week

Ask `how was my week`. Use it after you have logged some meals, training, check-ins or weigh-ins.

For a direct check against your goal, ask `am I on track`. Reports also opens with a short coach summary followed by your on-track card.

## Ask about patterns

Try `any patterns` when you want the coach to look across the information it has.

Patterns are clues, not proof. A change in sleep, food or training can happen at the same time without one causing the other. More complete logging gives the coach more to work with.

## Run an experiment

Ask `run an experiment` when you want to explore a change in your own routine.

Keep the question modest and observable. Give it enough time to produce useful information. Do not use an experiment to test symptoms, change medication, or push through pain.

> **Note:** Forma gives information, not medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional about medical decisions.

## Choose the amount of detail

Use the coach screen for a short answer. Open **Reports** from the grid menu for the summary and your fitness score, weight, muscle progress and body trends. Choose **Full report** for the full Progress screen.
