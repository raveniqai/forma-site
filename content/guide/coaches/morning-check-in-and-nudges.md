# Morning check-in and nudges

The morning check-in is a quick way to tell Forma how you feel. It takes a word or a number. You do not need to write a journal entry.

## Check in with a word

Choose the one that fits best:

- **Wrecked**
- **Flat**
- **Normal**
- **Good**
- **Strong**

There is no best answer. A low-energy morning is useful information too.

## Check in with a number

You can say or type `I feel 7`. Use the number that matches your morning rather than the number you think you should choose.

If you do not want to check in, you can carry on with something else. Log breakfast, look at the home numbers, or close the app.

## The optional morning nudge

Forma can give you an optional morning nudge. It is a gentle prompt, not another task list.

Turn it on if a small morning cue helps you. Leave it off if you prefer a quiet phone. The check-in and the nudge are separate choices: you can check in without wanting a notification.

## Look at the day afterwards

The home strip shows six quiet numbers: this week, protein, weight, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. Tap any one for its chart.

You can also ask the coach `how did I sleep this week` or `am I on track` when you want more than the numbers.

> **Tip:** If the nudge does not arrive, check that it is enabled and that iOS is allowed to send Forma notifications. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.
