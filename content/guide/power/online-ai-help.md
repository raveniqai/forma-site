# Online AI help

Forma Power adds optional online AI help. It can help with free-form questions, meal ideas, meal photos and meal descriptions.

It is opt-in. Forma Core keeps everything on your phone and does not require this online help.

## What you can use it for

### Free-form questions

Ask a broader question in your own words when you want online AI help with the wording or exploration.

The everyday coach requests in this guide, such as `log espresso` and `show my bench progress`, still begin on the same coach screen.

### Meal ideas

Ask for meal ideas when you want inspiration. Treat them as ideas to consider, not a medical diet or a prescription.

### Meal photos and descriptions

Use a meal photo or a written description when you want online AI help interpreting a meal. Check the result against what you actually ate before relying on it.

A photo can miss ingredients and portion details. A description can only use the detail you include.

## What is never sent from your watch

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service. This applies to original watch information and anything Forma derives from it.

Turning on online AI help does not change that boundary.

## Voice and consent

Speech recognition stays on the iPhone and is never sent anywhere. Choosing to use an online free-form question or meal request does not change where speech recognition happens.

If you do not want online AI help, leave it off and use Core. You can still talk or type to the coach.

> **Note:** AI meal estimates can be wrong. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
