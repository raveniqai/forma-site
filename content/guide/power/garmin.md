# Garmin data

Forma Power adds Garmin data. It can give the coach more context alongside the training, food and check-ins you record.

## Power and Core

Core keeps the Forma experience on your phone and does not include the Garmin addition.

Power is the plan to choose if you want Garmin data in Forma. Online AI help is a separate, opt-in part of Power.

## Sync your watch

On the coach screen, say or type `sync my watch` when you want to start a sync.

Afterwards, you can look at relevant home numbers and charts. Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate are all in the six-number strip.

You can also ask `how did I sleep this week` or `resting heart rate this month`.

## Other devices

Garmin is the first watch Forma connects to directly. Devices that save to Apple Health can share that way. More direct connections are planned. See [Other watches and rings](../other-devices/).

## Keep the numbers in context

Watch readings are one view of recovery. Read them alongside your morning check-in and how your training actually feels.

A number from a watch is not a diagnosis. Speak to a qualified professional about symptoms or readings that concern you.

## The AI boundary

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service. This remains true if you also opt into online AI help for free-form questions or meals.

Apple Health is optional and read-only as well. Forma never writes to Apple Health.
