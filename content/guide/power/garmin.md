# Garmin data

If you wear a Garmin watch, Forma can bring its sleep, recovery and training numbers into your plan. Your watch already measures what happens overnight, so Forma reads that instead of asking you to type anything. The training coach weighs it when it plans.

Garmin data is part of Forma Power. Core works without a watch: your targets, session plans, food logging and the recovery read from your logged sets all run as normal.

## When to use it

- You already wear a Garmin, and would rather Forma read it than enter sleep or rest days by hand.
- You want the Overnight card on Today to show your sleep, heart-rate variability (HRV, the variation between your heartbeats) and resting heart rate.
- You want the readiness and training load from your watch to count toward what the training coach plans next.
- You hold Forma Power. Without it the Garmin screen says "Garmin watch data is part of Power." and offers **See Power plans**.

## Step by step

1. Open **Settings**, then **Devices**, and tap **Garmin**.
2. If the card at the top already reads Connected, you are done. Otherwise tap **Connect Garmin**.
3. A sign-in sheet opens over Forma. Sign in on Garmin's own page inside it.
4. When the sheet closes, the Garmin screen says **Garmin connected.** If you stop halfway, it says **Connecting was cancelled. Nothing was changed.**
5. Wait for your watch to sync with Garmin. The screen then shows **Newest data** with the date of the latest reading. Before any data arrives it says **No data has arrived yet. It appears after your watch syncs.**
6. Open **Today**. The Overnight card shows your sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. The Recovery ring fills with the readiness figure from the watch when it sends one, or with the body battery figure when it does not.
7. To stop, tap **Disconnect Garmin** on the Garmin screen and confirm in the dialog. Forma stops receiving data from your watch, and you can connect again whenever you like.

> **Note:** You can tap **Check connection** on the Garmin screen at any time. It asks Forma's server what it knows and changes nothing on its own.

## What your watch adds

Garmin delivers your data to Forma's own server under Garmin's Open Wearables program. Forma keeps a copy on the phone, so the numbers are there when you open the app. What it brings:

- Last night's sleep: the time asleep and the sleep score, on the Overnight card.
- HRV and resting heart rate, compared with your own recent nights rather than a general norm.
- Today's stress, breathing and steps on the same card, shown while the newest night on the phone is older than last night.
- A sleep stage breakdown (Deep, Light, REM, Awake) on the Vitals screen in Progress.
- Training readiness, training load and your cardio sessions. The training coach reads them when it picks the next session and when it suggests rest.

> **Tip:** If the numbers look old, the usual reason is a watch that has not uploaded to Garmin yet. Sync it in Garmin's own app, then pull down on Today or reopen Forma and give it a moment.

## Good to know

> **Careful:** Nothing worked out from your Garmin data goes to an online AI service. The Garmin screen says: "Forma never sends it to online AI." Ask Coach answers questions about your watch data on the phone.

> **Note:** Connecting goes through Forma's server. The server holds the link to your Garmin account and knows a connection is active. When Garmin's sign-up for new connections is full, the screen says "Garmin connections are full right now; try again later."

> **Note:** You sign in on Garmin's own page inside the sheet. Forma never sees your Garmin password.

> **Note:** Disconnecting does not delete your history. Sessions, meals and weigh-ins stay exactly as they are, and the sleep and recovery rows already on the phone are not removed either.

> **Note:** If your Power subscription ends, the Garmin screen says **Paused. Garmin data already on this phone stays.** No new watch data arrives until you subscribe again.

> **Note:** Forma tells a failed sync apart from old data. If the last sync failed, the Overnight card says "Garmin sync failed" and keeps showing your last good data.
