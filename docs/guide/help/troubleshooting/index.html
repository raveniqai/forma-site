Troubleshooting

Simple checks for voice, nudges, watch sync, backup and surprising numbers.

On this page
  • Voice is not working well
  • The coach is not reading aloud
  • The morning nudge does not arrive
  • Watch data looks old
  • iCloud backup is not catching up
  • A target or chart looks wrong
  • Online AI help is unavailable
  • Related

Start with the simplest check. Most everyday requests can be typed if voice is not convenient, and your data controls are collected in Settings.

Voice is not working well

  1. Move somewhere quieter if you can.
  2. Use a short request with one action, such as log espresso.
  3. Type the same request on the coach screen instead.
  4. Ask what can you do if you are unsure how to phrase it.

Speech recognition happens on the iPhone and is never sent anywhere. Typing does not change the privacy of that recognition.

The coach is not reading aloud

The coach can read answers aloud. Check the iPhone's volume and any audio output you are using, then try again.

You can always read the answer silently. Voice output is optional.

The morning nudge does not arrive

Check that the optional morning nudge is enabled. Then check that iOS notifications for Forma are allowed and that a Focus mode is not holding them back.

You can still use the morning check-in without the nudge.

Watch data looks old

Say or type sync my watch. Then give the sync a chance to finish before checking the chart again.

Garmin data is part of Forma Power. Apple Health is optional and read-only. Review the relevant device or Health permission if you have changed it recently.

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

iCloud backup is not catching up

Open Settings > Data & backup and read the backup status.

Check that this iPhone is signed in to iCloud, has a connection, and has room for the backup. Your on-device data remains separate from the private iCloud copy.

A target or chart looks wrong

  1. Check the request or value you entered, especially decimal points and units.
  2. Ask why is my calorie target what it is.
  3. Review your goal and starting answers.
  4. Tap the relevant home number for its chart, or open Reports for the wider view.

Remember that targets, trends and the fitness score are estimates. Forma gives information, not medical advice.

Online AI help is unavailable

Online AI help is part of Forma Power and is opt-in. Check your plan and whether you have chosen to enable it.

Core's on-phone coaching is separate. You can continue to use everyday voice and typed requests without online AI help.
