Voice and privacy

Speech stays on the iPhone, and the coach can read answers aloud.

On this page
  • Talk or type
  • Listen to the coach
  • What online AI changes
  • Coach memories and backup
  • Related

You can speak to Forma on the coach screen. Speech is recognised on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Talk or type

Use voice when it is comfortable. Type the same request when you are in a meeting, on a train, or anywhere you would rather stay quiet.

For example, both ways work for requests such as:

  • log espresso
  • I weigh 78.4
  • start training
  • how did I sleep this week

You do not lose the coach-first experience by typing.

Listen to the coach

The coach can read answers aloud. Use this when listening is easier than reading.

You remain in control of when to use voice and when to read silently. Voice output does not change where your speech is recognised.

What online AI changes

Forma Core keeps everything on your phone. Forma Power adds optional online AI help for free-form questions, meal ideas, meal photos and meal descriptions.

Online AI help is opt-in. A free-form question or meal request you choose to send for online help is different from the speech recognition itself. Your speech is still recognised on the iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

Coach memories and backup

The few things your coach remembers stay on the phone. See and delete them under Settings > Data & backup > What the coach remembers.

iCloud backup uses your own private iCloud. Export and delete controls are in Settings.
