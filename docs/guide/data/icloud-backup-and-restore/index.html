iCloud backup

A backup in your own private iCloud, separate from online AI help.

On this page
  • Check your backup
  • What private iCloud means
  • Backup is not online AI
  • Before changing phones or deleting data
  • Related

Forma's iCloud backup uses your own private iCloud. It is your Apple Account's backup space, not a public copy of your Forma data.

Check your backup

Open Settings, then Data & backup, to find the backup controls and status.

Your iPhone needs access to iCloud for a backup to reach it. If the phone is offline, your on-device data is still there.

What private iCloud means

The backup belongs to your own iCloud account. Forma Core keeps everything on your phone, with this private backup rather than a Forma cloud account for your daily logs.

Apple Health remains optional and read-only. Coach memories are stored on the phone.

Backup is not online AI

Backing up to your private iCloud is separate from Forma Power's optional online AI help.

Online AI help is opt-in. Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

Before changing phones or deleting data

Check the backup status in Settings > Data & backup before making a major change.

If you want a separate copy, use the export control in the same area. Read deletion choices carefully so you know what will happen to the phone copy and the iCloud backup.

Tip: Do not send an export or health records to support unless you deliberately choose to share them.
