Export or delete all my data

Find the data controls in Settings and choose what happens to your records.

On this page
  • Export your data
  • Delete all your data
  • iCloud and Apple Health
  • Coach memories
  • Related

Forma puts export and deletion in Settings. You can take a copy of your data or remove it without contacting support.

Export your data

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Data & backup.
  3. Choose the export option.
  4. Follow the iPhone's sharing or saving flow.

Keep the export somewhere you trust. It can contain sensitive health, food and training information.

Delete all your data

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Data & backup.
  3. Choose Delete all my data.
  4. Read the confirmation carefully before proceeding.

Deletion is a bigger step than removing one coach memory. Use the memory controls if you only want to forget a food you avoid, being sick, travelling or a busy week.

Careful: Export first if you want a copy. Do not delete until you are sure you no longer need the data on this phone.

iCloud and Apple Health

Your iCloud backup is your own private iCloud copy. Read the choices shown when you delete so you understand what happens to it.

Apple Health keeps its own data and permissions. Forma only reads from Health and never writes to it.

Coach memories

To remove only saved coach context, open Settings > Data & backup > What the coach remembers. You can see and delete those memories there without choosing Delete all my data.
