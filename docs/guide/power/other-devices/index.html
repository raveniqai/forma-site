Other watches and rings

Garmin connects directly. Other devices can share through Apple Health. More are planned.

On this page
  • What works today
  • What is planned
  • Tell us what you use

What works today

  • Garmin connects directly. It is part of Forma Power. See Garmin data.
  • Anything that writes to Apple Health can share with Forma through Apple Health. If your watch, ring or scale saves sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, weight or activity to Apple Health, and you allow Forma to read it, those numbers can appear in your home strip and charts. What arrives depends on what your device saves to Apple Health. See Apple Health.

What is planned

More direct connections are planned. There is no date and no promised list yet.

Forma does not need a watch. Everything in Core works without one.

Tell us what you use

Write to support@raveniq.ai and say which device you would like to see. It helps us decide what to add next. Please do not send health data.

Something unclear or missing? Write to support@raveniq.ai and say which page.
