Setting up

Your first conversation, your targets and your first session.

On this page
  • What the coach asks
  • The reveal at the end
  • Your first conversation after setup
  • Privacy from the start
  • Related

The first run is a conversation with your coach. Answer a few basic questions in your own time. There is no separate setup maze to work through.

What the coach asks

The conversation covers:

  • your name;
  • your goal;
  • your height and weight;
  • your sex and birth date;
  • how many days you want to train;
  • where you train.

These answers give Forma a starting point for your targets and training. Give the answers that apply to you. If you are unsure about your training week, choose a number of days you can realistically keep.

The reveal at the end

The conversation ends with your starting plan. Forma reveals:

  • your calorie target;
  • your protein target;
  • your first session.

Read them before you move on. This is the beginning of the plan, not a test you have to pass.

Your first conversation after setup

You can keep using the same coach screen. Try start training when you are ready for the first session, or log something simple such as log espresso.

You do not have to use every feature on day one. A check-in, one meal and one training session are enough to begin.

Privacy from the start

Forma Core works on your phone. Speech is recognised on the iPhone and is never sent anywhere. Your iCloud backup, if you use it, belongs to your own private iCloud.

Apple Health is optional and read-only. Forma Power and its online AI help are optional too. Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

Note: Calorie and protein targets are estimates. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
