Log food by talking or typing

Tell the coach what you ate without opening a food form first.

On this page
  • Start with the meal and food
  • Log a drink
  • Log another item
  • With Forma Power
  • Related

The quickest way to log food is to tell the coach. Speak when that is convenient, or type the same words on the coach screen.

Start with the meal and food

Say or type log breakfast 107 g cucumber.

This gives the coach three useful pieces of information: that you are logging food, which meal it belongs to, and how much you had.

Use the amount you know. You do not need to turn your meal into a long description.

Log a drink

Say log espresso when that is all you need to record.

Small items can be logged in the same conversation as larger meals. There is no need to open a separate drinks screen.

Log another item

Keep going as you eat through the day. Each request can be short. log my creatine is there for your supplement record too.

Open Fuel from the grid menu at the top right when you want to review your food and targets in the full view.

With Forma Power

Power adds optional online AI help for meal ideas, meal photos and meal descriptions. It is opt-in.

You do not need Power for the everyday coach requests on this page. Speech recognition happens on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Tip: If speech is inconvenient in a public place, type the same request instead. The coach works either way.
