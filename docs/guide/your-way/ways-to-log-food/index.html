Ways to log your food

Speak, type, browse Fuel, or opt into meal help with Power.

On this page
  • Speak to the coach
  • Type to the coach
  • Open Fuel
  • Use optional meal help with Power
  • Related

Use the method that fits the moment. The coach screen keeps the everyday choices simple, and Fuel is under the grid menu when you want the full view.

Speak to the coach

Say what you ate, such as log breakfast 107 g cucumber or log espresso.

Speech is recognised on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. This is useful when your hands are busy and you can talk comfortably.

Type to the coach

Type the same request when you are somewhere quiet or want to check the wording and numbers first.

There is no separate typed command language. The examples in the guide work as written requests too.

Open Fuel

Open the grid menu at the top right and choose Fuel. Use it when you want to browse your food and targets rather than make a quick request.

You can move between the coach and Fuel as your needs change. The coach remains the main way into everyday logging.

Use optional meal help with Power

Forma Power adds opt-in online AI help for meal ideas, meal photos and meal descriptions.

Check meal estimates against the actual food and amount. Photos and descriptions can miss details. Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

MethodBest for
VoiceA quick log when you can speak
TypingQuiet places and checking exact numbers
FuelBrowsing the full food view
Power meal helpOptional ideas, photos and descriptions
