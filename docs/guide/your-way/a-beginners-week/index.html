A lighter way to begin

Build a useful routine without trying every feature at once.

On this page
  • Begin with one daily request
  • Add the morning check-in
  • Learn where the full screens are
  • End with a small review
  • Related

You do not need to master Forma in one week. Start with the few actions that make the app feel useful, then add more when you want them.

Begin with one daily request

Pick the request you are most likely to remember:

  • log espresso after a drink;
  • I weigh 78.4 after a weigh-in;
  • start training when it is time to train.

Speaking and typing both count. Choose whichever feels less like work.

Add the morning check-in

When you want to include it, choose Wrecked, Flat, Normal, Good or Strong. You can also say I feel 7.

Do not turn it into a test. A truthful Normal or Flat is more useful than an optimistic answer.

Learn where the full screens are

Open the grid menu at the top right. Find Train, Fuel and Reports once so you know where they are.

Then return to the coach. You do not need to browse those screens every day.

End with a small review

Ask how was my week or tap one of the six home numbers for its chart.

Keep what worked. If daily logging felt like too much, make next week lighter rather than giving up on the whole routine.
