Ways to use training

Start from the coach, browse Train, or ask for a smaller movement break.

On this page
  • Start with the coach
  • Open Train
  • Use movement around the session
  • Choose a small break instead
  • Related

Training can begin with one spoken or typed request. The full Train view and shorter movement options are close by when you want them.

Start with the coach

Say or type start training.

Before you begin, add context if the day needs it. I only have 35 minutes tells the coach your time limit. equipment taken? helps when your planned equipment is unavailable.

Open Train

Open the grid menu at the top right and choose Train when you prefer the full training view.

The coach is still there when you want to ask about the session or your progress later.

Use movement around the session

Ask for warm up before training and cool down afterwards. stretch is another option when you want a gentler finish.

These requests come from the same coach screen as start training.

Choose a small break instead

When a full session does not fit, ask for desk reset or stretch. A short break can be a more realistic choice on a crowded day.

Tell the coach about being sick, travelling or a busy week when that context matters. You can review those memories later on your phone.

Careful: Stop if something hurts. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
