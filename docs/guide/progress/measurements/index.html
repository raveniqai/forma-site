Recovery and sleep numbers

Read sleep, HRV and resting heart rate without chasing one perfect night.

On this page
  • Sleep
  • HRV
  • Resting heart rate
  • Where readings can come from
  • Related

Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate are three of the six quiet numbers on your home screen. Tap any one to open its chart.

Sleep

Ask how did I sleep this week for a week-level view.

A week is often more useful to review than a single night. Look at the information alongside how you felt in your morning check-ins.

HRV

HRV means heart-rate variability. It appears in the home strip with the other recovery numbers.

Tap HRV for its chart. Read it as one piece of information rather than a complete readiness diagnosis.

Resting heart rate

Ask resting heart rate this month when you want the monthly view. You can also tap resting heart rate in the home strip for its chart.

The longer view can help you avoid overreacting to one reading.

Where readings can come from

Apple Health is optional and read-only. Forma Power adds Garmin data. You can say sync my watch when you want the coach to start a watch sync.

Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.

Careful: Seek qualified medical advice about symptoms or readings that concern you. Forma gives information, not medical advice.
