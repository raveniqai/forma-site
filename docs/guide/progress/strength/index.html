Strength

Follow a lift, check records and look at training volume.

On this page
  • Follow a lift
  • Check new records
  • Check training volume
  • See muscle progress in Reports
  • Related

The coach gives you three direct ways to look at strength and training: a lift's progress, new records and training volume.

Follow a lift

Say or type show my bench progress.

You can use the name of another lift when that is the one you want to see. Give the chart enough history to show a direction rather than judging one isolated session.

Check new records

Ask any new records after a session or as part of your weekly review.

Records can be encouraging, but ordinary training without a record still counts. Recovery, sleep and life outside training can all add context to a quieter patch.

Check training volume

Ask show my training volume for a view of the work you have logged.

Volume does not tell you everything about quality or recovery. Read it with your coach summary, morning check-ins and recovery numbers when they are available.

See muscle progress in Reports

Open Reports from the grid menu. The on-track card includes muscle progress, along with your fitness score, weight and body trends.

Choose Full report for the full Progress screen.

Tip: Use how was my week when you want these separate training numbers brought back into a broader conversation.
