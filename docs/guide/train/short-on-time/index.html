Short on time

Tell the coach how long you have and keep the day realistic.

On this page
  • A simple short-on-time routine
  • When a session will not fit
  • When you are back on track
  • Related

You do not need to pretend a busy day is a normal one. Before training, say or type I only have 35 minutes.

Use the real amount of time you have. The coach conversation is the place to add that context.

A simple short-on-time routine

  1. Tell the coach your time limit.
  2. Say start training when you are ready.
  3. Ask equipment taken? if a busy space gets in the way.
  4. Leave a moment for cool down if you can.

Open Train from the grid menu if you prefer the full training view.

When a session will not fit

A small movement break is still a useful choice for the day. Ask for desk reset or stretch instead of forcing a full session into too little time.

If the whole week is crowded, tell the coach it is a busy week. That is one of the few things it can remember on your phone.

When you are back on track

There is no catch-up punishment. Return with start training, or ask how was my week when you want to review what happened.

Tip: Keep your request specific. A real time limit is more useful than saying only that you are busy.
