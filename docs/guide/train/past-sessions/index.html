Training history, records and volume

Ask what your training looks like over time.

On this page
  • Follow one lift
  • Check for records
  • Look at training volume
  • Use Reports for the wider picture
  • Related

Training progress is easier to see over several sessions than in one hard day. Ask the coach when you want the longer view.

Follow one lift

Say show my bench progress to look at bench. You can ask about another lift by using its name.

Look for the direction over time. One session can be affected by sleep, stress, equipment and many other ordinary things.

Check for records

Ask any new records after training or during a weekly review.

A record is worth noticing, but it does not have to be the goal of every session. Weeks with no new record still add to your history.

Look at training volume

Ask show my training volume when you want a view of the work you have done.

Volume is one part of the picture. Read it alongside how you feel, your recovery numbers and your other progress reports.

Use Reports for the wider picture

Open Reports from the grid menu. The on-track card includes muscle progress alongside your fitness score, weight and body trends. Choose Full report for the full Progress screen.

Note: Trends need history. Early on, the coach may have only a little to show.
