When equipment is taken

Ask the coach what to do when your planned spot is busy.

On this page
  • Ask while you are there
  • Use what you know about your training space
  • Choose a sensible alternative
  • Related

A busy gym does not have to end the session. When the equipment you expected to use is occupied, ask the coach equipment taken?.

Ask while you are there

Use the coach screen on your phone. You can speak if your hands are full, or type if the gym is loud.

Give the coach the context it asks for. Keep the request about what is available to you right now.

Use what you know about your training space

The first-run conversation asks where you train. That gives Forma a starting point before today's equipment problem comes up.

If you train somewhere different while travelling, tell the coach. Travel is one of the few pieces of context it can remember on your phone.

Choose a sensible alternative

An alternative should fit your body as well as your surroundings. Do not force a movement that hurts just to keep a session going.

Forma gives information, not medical advice. Ask a qualified professional about training with an injury.
