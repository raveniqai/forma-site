Training where you are

Your training location is part of your first conversation.

On this page
  • Answer for your normal week
  • When the setting changes
  • When space and time are limited
  • Related

Forma asks where you train during the first-run conversation. This helps the coach begin with your real setting rather than an imaginary gym.

Answer for your normal week

Choose the place you expect to use most often. Think about the equipment and space you can actually reach, not an ideal setup you rarely use.

Your answer about training days matters too. A realistic week and a realistic place give the coach a useful starting point.

When the setting changes

Tell the coach when you are travelling or when planned equipment is taken. These can be short messages, not a new setup routine.

Try equipment taken? when something is occupied. Tell the coach you are travelling when the whole training setting has changed.

When space and time are limited

Say I only have 35 minutes when time is the main constraint.

For a small movement break, ask for desk reset or stretch. These requests start from the same coach screen as a full training session.
