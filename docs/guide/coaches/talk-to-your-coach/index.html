Talk to your coach

A grouped cheat sheet of useful things to say or type.

On this page
  • Log food, supplements and weight
  • Train and adapt the session
  • Move and loosen up
  • Ask about recovery
  • Ask about training progress
  • Ask about your body and targets
  • Look for the wider picture
  • Ask about the coach and your devices
  • When you want more open-ended help
  • Related

Open Forma and use the coach screen. Speak or type in plain words. You do not need to open Train, Fuel or Reports first.

These examples are starting points, not special commands you have to memorise.

Log food, supplements and weight

  • log breakfast 107 g cucumber
  • log espresso
  • log my creatine
  • I weigh 78.4

Include the food and amount when you know them. Open Fuel from the grid menu when you want the full food view.

Train and adapt the session

  • start training
  • I only have 35 minutes
  • equipment taken?
  • warm up
  • cool down

Tell the coach about your time or equipment before you start when you can.

Move and loosen up

  • desk reset
  • stretch
  • warm up
  • cool down

Stop if anything hurts. Forma gives information, not medical advice.

Ask about recovery

  • how did I sleep this week
  • resting heart rate this month

You can also tap sleep, HRV or resting heart rate in the home-number strip to open a chart.

Ask about training progress

  • show my bench progress
  • any new records
  • show my training volume

Use the name of the lift you want to check. Trends become more useful as you build a history.

Ask about your body and targets

  • show my body fat
  • why is my calorie target what it is
  • am I on track

Reports brings your fitness score, weight, muscle progress and body trends together on the on-track card.

Look for the wider picture

  • any patterns
  • run an experiment
  • how was my week

Start with a question you care about. An experiment is a way to look at your own information, not a medical test.

Ask about the coach and your devices

  • what do you know about me
  • what can you do
  • sync my watch

To review or remove saved coach memories, open Settings, then Data & backup, then What the coach remembers.

When you want more open-ended help

Forma Power adds online AI help for free-form questions and meal ideas. It can also help with meal photos and descriptions. This help is opt-in.

Everyday coaching still works on your phone with Core. Nothing derived from your watch is ever sent to an AI service.
