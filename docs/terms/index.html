AI is optional. Where your iPhone offers Apple Intelligence, Forma can use it on the phone. With Forma Power, and only after you give your consent in Settings > AI help, Forma can also use an online AI service, through Forma's server, to read meal photos, estimate described meals and answer Ask Coach. Anything the AI reads or estimates is a draft for you to check, and it can be wrong. The online AI provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Garmin features are optional and need a Garmin watch and your own Garmin connection. Your use of Garmin is also subject to Garmin's own terms.
